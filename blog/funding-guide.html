Funding Your Study Abroad: A Comprehensive Guide

By InfoHub Finance Team June 10, 2023 8 min read

Understanding the Costs

Studying abroad is an investment in your future, but the initial price tag can often seem daunting. From tuition fees to living expenses, health insurance, and travel, the costs add up quickly. However, with the right strategy and resources, international education can be affordable for Zimbabwean students.

"The key to funding your studies abroad isn't just finding a big scholarship; it's about combining multiple sources of funding and managing your budget effectively."

1. Scholarships and Grants

Scholarships are the most sought-after form of funding. They don't need to be repaid and are usually based on academic merit, financial need, or specific talents.

  • Government Scholarships: Many countries offer scholarships for international students, such as the Chevening (UK), Fulbright (USA), and Vanier (Canada).
  • University-Specific Scholarships: Most universities have their own internal scholarships for international students. Always check the "Financial Aid" or "International Admissions" page of your target school.
  • Private Foundations: Organizations like the MasterCard Foundation offer full-ride scholarships for talented African students.

2. Financial Planning and Budgeting

Before you even apply, you need a clear picture of your finances. This involves:

  • Researching the cost of living in your destination city.
  • Accounting for currency exchange rate fluctuations.
  • Setting aside an emergency fund for unexpected expenses.

3. Alternative Funding Sources

If scholarships don't cover everything, consider:

  • Part-time Work: Many student visas allow you to work up to 20 hours per week during term time and full-time during holidays.
  • Education Loans: Some specialized lenders provide loans for international students without a co-signer in the host country.
  • Crowdfunding and Community Support: Sometimes, family and community support can bridge the final gap in your funding.
