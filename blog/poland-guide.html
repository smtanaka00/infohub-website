Study in Poland: Your Complete Application Guide

By European Desk August 12, 2023 7 min read

Why Poland?

Poland has emerged as a top destination for Zimbabwean students looking for high-quality education at an affordable price. With a rich history, vibrant culture, and world-class universities, Poland offers a unique European experience without the high cost of living found in the West.

"Affordability meets excellence in Poland, making it the perfect gateway to a global career."

1. Choosing Your University

Poland has over 500 higher education institutions. Some of the most popular among international students include:

  • University of Warsaw: The largest and most prestigious university in Poland.
  • Jagiellonian University: One of the oldest universities in the world, located in the beautiful city of Krakow.
  • Warsaw University of Technology: Ideal for students interested in engineering and applied sciences.

2. Application Requirements

Most Polish universities follow a similar application process:

  • High school diploma or Bachelor's degree (legalized/apostilled).
  • Transcripts of records.
  • Proof of English proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL, or a university internal test).
  • Copy of your passport.
  • Health certificate and insurance.

3. Tuition Fees and Living Costs

Tuition fees in Poland are remarkably low, often ranging from €2,000 to €4,000 per year for English-taught programs. Living expenses (accommodation, food, transport) are also affordable, typically around €400 to €600 per month.

4. Student Visa Process

Once you have your acceptance letter, you'll need to apply for a Type D national visa. This allows you to stay in Poland for more than 90 days and even travel within the Schengen Area.
